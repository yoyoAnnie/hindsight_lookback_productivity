# Session Log - 2026-10-16 - Plain language rewrite

- **Goal (set before the work):** rewrite the project docs in a blunt,
  plain voice, at the owner's call. Keep all facts and values. Cut
  decoration.
- **Tools used:** Muse (work session).
- **First prompt used:** n/a. This is a docs session.

## What was done

- The owner said the text read as AI text and as fluff. She set a new
  rule: write like a developer who talks to a viewer or a future
  recruiter face to face. Aim for clear logic, not decoration.
- README and docs 01 to 11 rewritten in that voice.
- The rule went into `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `docs/05`, and `docs/07`. It
  also went into the agent's own rule files, so new sessions keep it.

## Rules in the rewrite

- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Plain words. The plain word wins if one works.
- Basic link words only: and, but, so, because, then, if, when.
- Cut big link words: however, therefore, furthermore, moreover,
  in addition, as a result, nevertheless.
- Say the fact. Give the number, the name, or the step.

## What changed in the product

- Nothing. Facts, hex values, motion values, rules, and scope stayed
  the same. Text got shorter in most docs.
