# 01 - Vision and Concept

## The problem I hit

I do creative work in many apps at once. One idea for one project can
sit in five places:

- a file or a report in a drive
- feedback in a chat
- a daily note in a notes app
- a sketch on paper, shot with a phone and then lost
- a goal in my head, never drawn or built

The pain is the switch between apps. The pain is not a lack of tools.
I lost ideas between tools. The people I talked to told me the same.

## The hard truth under the problem

More facts were meant to make work faster. AI tools do make it easy
to find new ideas. But the users I talked to said the work got slower,
not faster. I see the same thing in my own work.

The reason is plain. New ideas are easy to get. It is hard to cut the
list down. We save links and files and then never open them. We keep
looking for new ideas. We do not work on the ones we saved. Each new
save pulls us away from the goal.

This is a known pattern in my field, psychology. It is called
information foraging. Saved files have a weak pull, like a weak
smell. A fresh feed has a strong pull. And it costs nothing to jump
to the fresh feed. So we jump, day after day.

So Hindsight has no feed for new ideas. And it counts progress only
when I act on a Work. It never counts saves as progress. The full
plan is in `11-garden-and-anti-foraging.md`.

## The idea

Hindsight is a scrapbook journal for ideas. It has one stream. I keep,
direct, and see my work in that one stream.

I do not sort work by folder. I sort work by what I do with it:

- **Hand - Projects (Hold):** the stuff itself. Files, scripts,
  transcripts, podcast notes, reports, images.
- **Brain - Notes (Direct):** my daily notes on that stuff. My notes
  hold what stuck, what people said, and the one change I want today.
- **Eye - Visualize (See):** the same stuff drawn in a clear form. A
  mind map for an idea. A prototype for a product. A layout view for
  a piece of art. A concept map for study. I can then judge the work.
  Is it rich? Is it clear? Is it good to look at?

The name is the point. All my work stays on one timeline. So a look
back is of use. That look back is hindsight.

## Who I build it for first

People who think in many forms and work over days, not in one sit.
I am one of them:

- creators who build content (video, podcast, writing) from source files
- students who turn thick source files into clear knowledge over weeks
- makers and artists who build one piece in many versions

More user types can come later. I do not plan for them in the first
version.

## What makes it not the same as other tools

1. **One object, not three apps.** The file, my notes on it, and the
   map of it are views of one Work. The app copies nothing between places.
2. **A daily loop, not a bin.** The core act is small and daily: one
   note of what stuck, one change. I did not build it for save and forget.
3. **Seeing is a test.** The Eye is not a nice extra. It tells me what
   is thin, what is not linked, and what clashes.
4. **A human feel.** Dates I can read. Notes in the margin. Marks I
   made stay in view. Plain words in all copy.

## What it is not

- Not a notes app for all use. Not a file drive. Not a task board.
- Not an AI that thinks for me. The Brain is my own mind. The machine
  can split files, link parts, draw maps, and flag gaps. It does not
  pick my daily change.
- Not social in the first version. My work is private by default.
  See `08-privacy-and-ip.md`.

## Style in one line

Old painting warmth and collage, with the clear layout of Notion.
Fast, smooth scroll motion is the mark of taste. Full values are in
`05-design-system.md`.

## What good looks like

I open Hindsight each day. I work one Work through the loop. I can
point at a map or a prototype and say if the idea got better this week.
